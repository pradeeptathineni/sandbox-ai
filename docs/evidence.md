# Evidence and decisions

Keep these meanings separate. A record can contain several without turning them into one status.

| Meaning | Required support | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw observation | Original artifact identity, timestamp and observed operation | Describes what happened in that scope. |
| Claim | Explicit statement, supporting references and uncertainty | Source support does not establish general fit. |
| Independent review | Separate reviewer, method, inputs and findings | Self-review and another pass by the author are not independent. |
| Provisional decision | Decision owner, intended consumer, rationale, dissent and revisit trigger | A recommendation grants no execution or adoption authority. |
| Adopted result | Authorized owner, actual destination, exact selected version and adoption record | A proposed disposition or passing check is not deployment. |
| Unsuccessful or unknown trial | Attempt status, failure layer, preserved receipt and missing measurements | Failure, interruption, blocked access and missing counters remain visible. |

Use attempted, completed, partial, failed, blocked, cancelled or not-run for execution. Keep unknown outcomes explicit. Record model, harness, evaluator, tool and infrastructure failures separately when their cause is known.

Choose metrics from the decision's actual acceptance criteria. Record units, denominator, source, exclusions and uncertainty beside each value. Null means unknown, not zero. Cumulative snapshots must not be summed as independent events. Cached or reasoning breakdowns are not extra tokens to add again. Estimated API prices are not billed subscription cost. Requested and served model identities remain separate. Bytes or file counts do not measure tokens, peak context or human value.

Compare a strong native baseline with the candidate under equivalent inputs, permissions and stopping criteria. Preserve exclusions and every attempted arm. Repeat only when variation could change the choice, and report distributions when warranted. Unequal case counts and concurrent work do not establish a causal timing advantage. Freeze the rubric before grading and retain grader errors alongside valid outcomes.

A useful result lets a decision owner find the evidence, understand its limits and make a scoped choice. Mechanical validation, installed resources, observed use, independent checking and human usefulness are separate evidence classes.

Private evidence stays in its original owner. A portable record uses reviewed public or synthetic data. Before any future move, agree ownership, preserve hashes and attribution, keep a recoverable original, repair references, and verify both sides. Link immutable receipts when a move adds no value.
